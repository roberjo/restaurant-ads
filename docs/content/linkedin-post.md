I started with a simple idea: put ads from local businesses on the TVs inside restaurants.

Before writing any code, I asked my AI coding partner (Claude Code) to tear the idea apart. It did:

📺 Most restaurant TVs show live sports, and you can't put ads into a cable broadcast. The real inventory is idle time and menu boards.
📱 Roku and Samsung apps can't force themselves to start or stay on screen. A $40 kiosk stick is the reliable path.
🏁 Well-funded players already do the national version.
📉 One screen earns very little. It only works with density.

So I changed the plan. Instead of running an ad network myself, I'm building the platform that lets local operators (AV installers, agencies) run their own, while every operator's screens are pooled into one network that national buyers care about.

The research also turned up something I hadn't considered: selling a "turnkey business" can legally make you a franchise. That one finding shaped product decisions such as white-labelling and usage-based fees.

What exists today (v0.1.0):
✅ Multi-tenant platform for operators, venues and advertisers
✅ Pairing a TV takes a 6-character code
✅ Free menu boards with local ads mixed in
✅ Proof-of-play with fraud checks, plus revenue share calculated to the micro-dollar
✅ 20 automated tests, CodeQL, Trivy and secret scanning, and SHA-pinned CI/CD

The biggest lesson: ask to be challenged first. The most valuable output of day one wasn't code. It was being told where my idea was weakest.

Next up: payouts, a kiosk player, and POS-synced menus.

If you install AV in restaurants or sell local advertising, I'd love to talk. I'm looking for 2–3 design partners.
